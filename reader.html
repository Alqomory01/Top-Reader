3.2 — Newton's Laws of Motion

Every object in a state of uniform motion tends to remain in that state unless an external force is applied to it. This forms the cornerstone of classical mechanics.

The net force on an object is equal to the rate of change of its momentum — this is the fundamental relationship governing all mechanical systems.

These principles are essential for understanding everything from projectile motion to orbital mechanics, and will be applied throughout this course.
